# Team members, roles and account security

Add staff so they can work in Kuberix for Business without your login.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including bank account changes and adding admins |
| Admin | Everything except changing the bank account or the owner |
| Accountant | View payments, settlements and reports; download invoices |
| Developer | Manage API keys and webhooks; view payments |
| Cashier | See payments to their assigned QR code only |

## Add a team member

1. Go to **Account > Team > Invite**.
2. Enter their mobile number or email and choose a role.

They get an invitation that expires in 7 days.

## Remove someone

Go to **Account > Team**, tap the person and choose **Remove**. Their access ends immediately. If they were a Developer, also regenerate your API keys.

## Keep the account safe

- **Two-step verification** is required for Owners and Admins: an OTP on every new device.
- Bank account changes need the **Owner's OTP** and take effect after a **24-hour** safety wait.
- Kuberix will never ask for your OTP or password by phone.
