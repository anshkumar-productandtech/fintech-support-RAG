# Pay bills and check bill payment status

Kuberix pays electricity, water, gas, broadband, postpaid mobile, FASTag, insurance premium and loan EMI bills through **Bharat BillPay (BBPS)**.

## Pay a bill

1. Tap **Bills** and choose a category.
2. Choose your biller and enter your customer or consumer number.
3. Kuberix fetches your bill. Check the amount and due date, then pay.

Every BBPS payment gets a **BBPS reference ID**. It's your proof of payment, even if the biller hasn't updated their records yet.

## Biller still shows the bill as unpaid

Billers can take up to **3 working days** to update. Your BBPS receipt counts as proof of payment. If you're charged a late fee because the biller didn't update in time, raise an issue in the app with the BBPS reference ID.

## Bill payment pending or failed

- **Pending:** wait up to 3 working days for the biller to confirm.
- **Failed:** refunded automatically. UPI and wallet payments come back within T + 1 day; cards within 5 to 7 working days.

## Raise a BBPS complaint

Tap the payment in **History > Raise an issue**. Kuberix registers it on BBPS and gives you a **complaint ID**. BBPS complaints are usually resolved within 7 working days.
