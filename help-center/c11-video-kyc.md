# Complete your KYC with Video KYC

Video KYC upgrades your Kuberix Wallet to Full KYC in about 5 minutes, on a video call with a Kuberix agent.

## Before you start

- Your original **PAN card** (a photo or photocopy won't work)
- Your **Aadhaar number**, with your mobile number linked to it for OTP
- A plain sheet of white paper and a pen, to sign on camera
- Good light, a stable internet connection, and a quiet place
- You must be in India during the call

## Steps

1. Go to **Wallet > Upgrade to Full KYC > Start Video KYC**.
2. Enter your Aadhaar number and the OTP.
3. Wait to be connected to an agent. Calls run from **9 am to 9 pm, every day**.
4. Show your PAN card to the camera, sign the paper, and answer a few questions.

## After the call

Most upgrades are approved within 24 hours. You'll get a notification.

## Why was my Video KYC rejected?

Common reasons: the PAN card was blurry or not the original, the name on your PAN and Aadhaar don't match, your face wasn't clearly visible, or the call dropped. You can try again after 24 hours.
