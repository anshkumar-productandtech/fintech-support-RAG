# Set up or cancel AutoPay (UPI mandates)

AutoPay lets a merchant charge you automatically on a schedule, for example a monthly streaming subscription or a SIP. You approve it once with your UPI PIN.

## Set up AutoPay

You usually start it from the merchant's website or app by choosing **UPI AutoPay**. Kuberix then shows you a request with the merchant, the maximum amount and how often they can charge you. Approve it with your UPI PIN.

## Before each payment

- You get a **notification at least 24 hours before** each charge, with the amount and date.
- For payments **above ₹15,000**, you're asked to approve each one with your UPI PIN.

## See, pause or cancel AutoPay

Go to **Profile > AutoPay**. Tap a mandate to:

- **Pause** it for a period you choose
- **Cancel** it permanently

Cancelling stops future charges. It doesn't refund past payments; contact the merchant for those.

## Charged after you cancelled?

Raise an issue from **History** with the payment. If money was taken after a valid cancellation, your bank must reverse it.
