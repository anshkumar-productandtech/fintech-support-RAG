# Kuberix Rewards and cashback

## How rewards work

Some payments earn a **scratch card** after they succeed. Scratch it in **Rewards** to see what you've won: cashback, coins or a voucher.

## Cashback

- Credited to your **Kuberix Wallet** within **48 hours** of scratching.
- Cashback in the wallet doesn't expire, but your wallet KYC limits apply.

## Scratch cards and coins

- Scratch cards expire **30 days** after you receive them.
- **Kuberix Coins** can be exchanged for vouchers in **Rewards > Store**. Coins expire 12 months after you earn them.

## Why didn't I get a reward?

Rewards apply only to offers you see in the app before paying. Common reasons for no reward:

- The payment didn't meet the offer's minimum amount
- The offer was already used the maximum number of times
- The payment was refunded or cancelled
- The payment was to yourself or between your own accounts

If a cashback you scratched hasn't arrived after 48 hours, raise an issue from **Rewards > History**.
