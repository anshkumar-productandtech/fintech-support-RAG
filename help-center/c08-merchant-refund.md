# When will I get a refund from a shop or website?

When a merchant refunds you (for example, after you return an order), the refund goes back to the way you paid.

## Refund timelines after the merchant starts the refund

| You paid with | Refund reaches you in |
| --- | --- |
| Kuberix Wallet | Instantly |
| UPI | 1 to 3 working days |
| Debit card | 5 to 7 working days |
| Credit card | 5 to 7 working days, shown on your card statement |
| Net banking | 5 to 7 working days |

## The merchant says they refunded, but I don't see it

1. Ask the merchant for the **refund reference number** (sometimes called ARN or RRN).
2. Check your bank or card statement for that number. Refunds sometimes show as a credit with a different description.
3. If the timeline above has passed, go to **History**, tap the original payment, then **Raise an issue > Refund not received**.

## The merchant won't refund me

Kuberix can't force a merchant to refund you. Contact the merchant first. If you paid a Kuberix merchant and they don't respond within 7 days, raise an issue in the app and we'll contact them.

## Not a refund?

If your payment **failed** and money was debited, that's different. See "Money was debited but my payment failed".
