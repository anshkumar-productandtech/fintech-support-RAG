# Sign up for Kuberix for Business

Kuberix for Business lets you accept UPI, card, wallet and net banking payments in your shop or online.

## Documents you need

| Business type | Documents |
| --- | --- |
| Individual or sole proprietor | PAN, Aadhaar, a bank account in your or your business's name, and one business proof (GST certificate, Udyam registration or shop licence) |
| Partnership or LLP | Business PAN, partnership deed or LLP agreement, bank account in the business's name, and PAN and Aadhaar of one authorised partner |
| Private or public limited company | Company PAN, Certificate of Incorporation, bank account in the company's name, and PAN and Aadhaar of one authorised director |

GST registration isn't required to sign up. If your business is registered for GST, add your GSTIN in **Account > Business details** to get GST invoices for Kuberix fees and higher collection limits.

## Steps

1. Download the **Kuberix for Business** app or go to **business.kuberix.example**.
2. Enter your mobile number and business details.
3. Upload the documents and complete a short video verification.

## How long does approval take?

Usually **2 working days**. Until you're fully approved, you can accept up to **₹1,00,000 per day** by QR code. Online payments start after approval.

## Rejected?

The rejection email lists the reason. The most common ones are a bank account name that doesn't match the business, or a blurry document. Fix it and resubmit from **Account > Verification**.
