# When will I receive my money? (Settlements)

A **settlement** is when Kuberix sends the payments you've collected to your bank account.

## Standard settlement

| You collected | Money in your bank |
| --- | --- |
| Monday to Friday | Next working day, by 1 pm |
| Saturday, Sunday or a bank holiday | Next working day after that, by 1 pm |

Payments are counted from midnight to midnight. Rentals and fees (for example Soundbox rental) are deducted before settlement.

## Instant settlement

Turn on **Settlements > Instant settlement** to get money in your bank within **15 minutes, 24x7, including holidays**.

- Fee: **0.2%** of the amount settled, plus GST
- Minimum per settlement: ₹100
- Not available for the first 30 days after you sign up

## See your settlements

**Settlements** in the app shows each settlement with its **UTR number**. Your bank uses the UTR to trace the deposit.

## Settlement looks smaller than expected?

The settlement report lists every payment, refund, chargeback and fee deducted. See "Fees and charges" and "Refund a customer".
