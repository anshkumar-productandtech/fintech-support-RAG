# Close your Kuberix account

## Before you close

- **Empty your Kuberix Wallet.** Full KYC users can transfer the balance to their bank. Minimum KYC users can spend it at merchants, or contact support to return it to the source.
- **Cancel all AutoPay mandates** in **Profile > AutoPay**.
- **Turn off UPI Lite** so the balance goes back to your bank.
- Download any statements you need (see "Transaction history and statements").

## Close your account

Go to **Profile > Settings > Close account** and follow the steps. Your account closes **30 days** after the request. Log in during those 30 days to cancel the request.

## What happens to your data

After closing, Kuberix keeps your transaction records for the period required by law. Your profile is removed from the app and you won't receive marketing messages.

## Only want to remove a bank account?

Go to **Profile > Bank accounts**, tap the account and choose **Remove**. This doesn't close your Kuberix account.
