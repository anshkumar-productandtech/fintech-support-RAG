# Collection limits and how to raise them

Limits protect you and your customers from fraud. They depend on how far your verification has gone.

| Account stage | Daily collection limit | Monthly limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signed up, verification pending | ₹1,00,000 (QR only) | ₹5,00,000 |
| Verified individual or proprietor | ₹5,00,000 | ₹50,00,000 |
| Verified business with GST | ₹25,00,000 | No monthly limit |

## What happens when I reach the limit

New payments are **declined** for the rest of the day (or month). Customers see "Merchant limit reached". Payments already received are safe.

## Raise your limit

Go to **Account > Limits > Request increase** and upload:

- GST certificate, if you have one
- Bank statements for the last 6 months
- For online businesses, your website with refund, cancellation and contact pages

Requests are reviewed within **3 working days**.

## Temporary increase

For a sale or a festival season, request a temporary increase up to 30 days long. Ask at least 3 working days before you need it.
