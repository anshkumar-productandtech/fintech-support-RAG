# Report fraud or an unauthorised payment

Act fast. The sooner you report, the better the chance of stopping the money.

## Do these right now

1. **Block your Kuberix account:** go to **Help > Block my account**. If you can't open the app, use **kuberix.example/block** from any browser.
2. **Call your bank** and ask them to block UPI on your account.
3. **Report it to the National Cybercrime Helpline: call 1930** or file a complaint at **cybercrime.gov.in**.
4. **Report it to Kuberix:** go to **History**, tap the payment, then **Report fraud**.

## Report within 3 days

If you report an unauthorised payment to Kuberix **within 3 days**, and you didn't share your PIN, OTP or password, your liability for a Kuberix Wallet payment is zero. Reports made later are reviewed case by case.

For UPI payments from your bank account, your bank decides refunds under its own policy. Report to your bank as soon as possible.

## Common scams

- Someone asks you to **enter your UPI PIN to receive money**. You never need a PIN to receive money.
- A caller says they're from Kuberix and asks for an OTP, or asks you to install a screen-sharing app.
- A "refund" or "cashback" link that asks you to log in.

Kuberix will never ask for your PIN, OTP or password.
