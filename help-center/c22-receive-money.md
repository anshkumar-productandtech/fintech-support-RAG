# Receive money and request money

## Receive money

Share your **UPI ID** (for example `asha@kbx`) or your **QR code** from **Profile > My QR**. Money arrives directly in your primary bank account. You **never** need to enter a UPI PIN to receive money.

## Request money

1. Tap **Send money**, pick the person, then **Request**.
2. Enter the amount and a note.

They get a request in their UPI app and pay with their UPI PIN. Requests expire after **7 days**.

## Didn't receive money someone sent?

1. Ask the sender for the **UPI reference number** and check that the payment shows **Successful** for them.
2. Check your bank statement, not just Kuberix. The money goes to your primary bank account.
3. Check which account is primary in **Profile > Bank accounts**.

If the sender's payment is successful and it's not in your bank account after 24 hours, contact your bank with the UPI reference number.

## Block requests

If someone keeps sending you requests, tap the request and choose **Block**. Never approve a request you don't recognise; approving a request **sends** money.
