# GST invoices for Kuberix fees

Kuberix issues a tax invoice every month for the fees and GST it charged you.

## When and where

- Invoices for each month are ready by the **7th of the next month**.
- Download them from **Reports > Invoices**. Owners and Accountants get them by email too.

## Claim input tax credit

To claim input tax credit, your **GSTIN** must be on your account **before** the month starts. Add it in **Account > Business details**. Kuberix can't reissue past invoices with a GSTIN added later.

## What the invoice shows

- Fees for each payment method
- Soundbox rental and other charges
- GST charged (CGST and SGST, or IGST)

## Something wrong on an invoice?

Raise a ticket from **Help > Invoice issue** within **30 days** of the invoice date.
