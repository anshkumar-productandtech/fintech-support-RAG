# Add money to or withdraw from Kuberix Wallet

## Add money

1. Tap **Wallet > Add money**.
2. Enter the amount.
3. Choose how to pay: UPI, debit card, net banking or credit card.

| Method | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| UPI | Free |
| Debit card | Free |
| Net banking | Free |
| Credit card | 2% of the amount |

Money added by UPI or debit card shows up instantly. Net banking can take up to 30 minutes.

## Withdraw to your bank account (Full KYC only)

1. Tap **Wallet > Transfer to bank**.
2. Choose your linked bank account and enter the amount.

Transfers are free and usually reach your bank within 2 hours, at the latest by the next working day.

## Money added but not in the wallet?

If your bank was debited but the wallet wasn't credited, the money comes back to your bank within T + 1 day. If it doesn't, raise an issue from **History**.
