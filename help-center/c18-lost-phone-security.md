# Lost phone, new phone or changed SIM

## Lost or stolen phone

1. Block your account at **kuberix.example/block** from any browser.
2. Ask your mobile operator to block your SIM, then get a replacement SIM with the same number.
3. Ask your bank to block UPI until you're set up again.

Your money stays safe in your bank account. Nobody can make UPI payments without your UPI PIN.

## Setting up on a new phone

1. Install Kuberix and log in with your mobile number.
2. Kuberix verifies your SIM by SMS again, then asks you to re-link your bank account.
3. Your UPI PIN stays the same.

For your safety, the **first 24 hours on a new phone** have a total UPI limit of **₹5,000**.

## Changed your mobile number?

Update the new number with your bank first. Then log in to Kuberix with the new number and link your bank account again. Your old Kuberix account and wallet balance can be moved to the new number by **Help > Chat with us**; we'll verify your identity first.

## App lock

Turn on **Profile > Security > App lock** to require your phone's fingerprint, face or passcode every time you open Kuberix.
