# Complaints and escalation

## Level 1: Kuberix Support

Raise an issue from the payment in **History**, or go to **Help > Chat with us**. Chat is open **24 hours a day** for payment issues. You'll get a ticket number. Most issues are resolved within **3 working days**.

You can also email **support@kuberix.example** with your ticket number.

## Level 2: Grievance Officer

If you're not happy with the answer, or there's no answer within 7 working days, write to the Kuberix Grievance Officer at **grievance@kuberix.example**. Include your ticket number. The Grievance Officer replies within **10 working days**.

## Level 3: RBI Ombudsman

If your complaint isn't resolved within **30 days**, or you're not satisfied with the final reply, you can complain to the RBI Ombudsman at **cms.rbi.org.in**. It's free.

## Tips for a faster answer

- Always include the **UPI reference number**, or the BBPS reference ID for bills.
- One ticket per problem. Opening several tickets for the same payment slows things down.
