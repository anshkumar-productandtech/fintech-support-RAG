# Fees and charges for merchants

## Payment fees (MDR)

| Payment method | Fee per payment |
| --- | --- |
| UPI from a bank account | 0% |
| RuPay debit card | 0% |
| Other debit cards | 0.9% |
| Credit cards (domestic) | 1.99% |
| Credit card on UPI (RuPay credit) | 1.5% for payments above ₹2,000; 0% up to ₹2,000 |
| Net banking | 1.9% |
| Wallets | 1.9% |
| International cards | 2.99% |

GST at **18%** is added to every fee. There's no fee on refunds, but the original fee isn't returned when you refund a payment.

## Other charges

| Item | Charge |
| --- | --- |
| Account setup | Free |
| Annual maintenance | Free |
| Soundbox rental | ₹125 per month |
| Instant settlement | 0.2% of the amount settled |
| Chargeback handling | ₹250 per chargeback you lose |

## Where fees are taken

Fees are deducted from your settlement before it reaches your bank. The **settlement report** shows the fee and GST for every payment.

## Custom pricing

Businesses collecting more than **₹50 lakh a month** online can ask for custom pricing from **Help > Talk to sales**.
