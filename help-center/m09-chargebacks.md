# Chargebacks and payment disputes

A **chargeback** happens when a customer asks their bank to reverse a payment, for example because they say they didn't receive the order or didn't make the payment.

## What happens

1. Kuberix emails you and shows the chargeback in **Disputes**, with the reason and the deadline.
2. The disputed amount is **held from your settlements** while the case is open.
3. You **accept** it (the customer gets the money back) or **contest** it with evidence.

## Respond within 5 calendar days

If you don't respond by the deadline, the chargeback is accepted automatically and you lose the amount.

## Evidence that helps

- Invoice or order confirmation
- Proof of delivery with date and signature, or delivery tracking
- For services: proof the service was used, such as login records
- Your communication with the customer
- Your refund and cancellation policy, as shown to the customer

## How long it takes

The customer's bank decides. Most cases close within **30 to 45 days**.

- **You win:** the held amount is released in your next settlement.
- **You lose:** the amount goes to the customer, plus a **₹250** chargeback fee.

## Already refunded the customer?

Upload the refund reference number as evidence. You won't pay twice.
