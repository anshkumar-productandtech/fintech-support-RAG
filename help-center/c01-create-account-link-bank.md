# Create your Kuberix account and link your bank account

You need three things to start: a phone with the Kuberix app, the mobile number registered with your bank, and a debit card or Aadhaar number for that bank account.

## Steps

1. Download the Kuberix app and open it.
2. Enter your mobile number. It must be the same number your bank has on record.
3. Allow Kuberix to send an SMS from your phone. This verifies that the SIM is inside this phone. Keep the SIM in the phone while you use UPI.
4. Choose your bank from the list. Kuberix finds the accounts linked to your mobile number.
5. Pick the account you want to use.
6. Set your UPI PIN if this is your first time using UPI with this account (see "Set or reset your UPI PIN").

Your UPI ID is created automatically in the format `yourname@kbx`. You can change it once every 30 days in **Profile > UPI IDs**.

## If your bank account doesn't show up

- Check that the SIM in this phone has the number registered with your bank. If you use two SIMs, choose the right SIM slot.
- Make sure the SIM has an active plan and can send SMS. Kuberix can't verify a SIM that has no outgoing SMS balance.
- Ask your bank to confirm that your mobile number is linked to the account and that the account is active.

## Linking more accounts

You can link up to 5 bank accounts. Go to **Profile > Bank accounts > Add bank account**. Choose one as your primary account; money you receive on your UPI ID goes there.

## Still need help?

Go to **Help > Chat with us** in the app. Never share your UPI PIN, OTP or card details with anyone, including people who say they work for Kuberix.
