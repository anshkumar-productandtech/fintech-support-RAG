# Send money with UPI

You can send money to a contact, a UPI ID, a bank account, or by scanning a QR code.

## Ways to pay

- **To a contact:** tap **Send money**, pick the contact. If they use any UPI app, you'll see their name.
- **To a UPI ID:** tap **Send money > UPI ID** and type it, for example `priya@kbx`.
- **To a bank account:** tap **Send money > Bank account** and enter the account number and IFSC.
- **By QR code:** tap **Scan**, point your camera at the QR code.

Then enter the amount, add a note if you like, and approve with your UPI PIN.

## Check the name before you pay

Before you enter your PIN, Kuberix shows the name registered with the receiver's bank. **If the name doesn't match the person you mean to pay, stop.** UPI payments can't be cancelled once approved.

## How long it takes

Most UPI payments complete within seconds. If a payment shows **Pending**, see "My payment is pending".

## Fees

Kuberix doesn't charge you for sending money with UPI from your bank account.
