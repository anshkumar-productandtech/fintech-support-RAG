# Kuberix QR code and Soundbox

## QR code

Your QR code is free. Download and print it from **Account > My QR**, or order a printed standee from **Store** (₹199, delivered in 5 to 7 days). Customers can scan it with **any UPI app**.

## Soundbox

The Kuberix Soundbox announces every payment out loud, so you don't need to check your phone.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Rental | ₹125 per month, charged from your settlement |
| Security deposit | ₹499, refunded when you return it |
| Connection | Built-in 4G SIM, no Wi-Fi needed |
| Battery | About 3 days per charge |
| Languages | 11 Indian languages, including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and Marathi |

Order it from **Store > Soundbox**. It arrives in 3 to 5 working days, already linked to your QR.

## Change the announcement language

Press and hold the **M** button for 3 seconds, then press **+** until you hear your language.

## Return or cancel

Request a pickup from **Store > My devices > Return**. Your deposit is refunded within 7 working days after we receive the device in working condition.
