# Transaction history and statements

## See your payments

Tap **History**. Filter by date, status (Successful, Pending, Failed), or type (UPI, Wallet, Bills, Recharges). Tap any payment to see its receipt, including the **UPI reference number**.

## Download a statement

1. Go to **History > Download statement**.
2. Choose a date range of up to **12 months** at a time.
3. Kuberix emails a password-protected PDF to your registered email. The password is the first 4 letters of your name in capitals followed by your date of birth as DDMM.

Statements cover your **Kuberix Wallet** and all payments made through the Kuberix app. For a full bank account statement, use your bank's app.

## Payment missing from History?

Payments made from other UPI apps don't show in Kuberix History, even if they used the same bank account.
