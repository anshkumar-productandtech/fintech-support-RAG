# My mobile or DTH recharge failed

## If the recharge shows Failed

Your money is refunded automatically:

- Paid by Kuberix Wallet: back in your wallet instantly
- Paid by UPI: back in your bank account within T + 1 day
- Paid by card or net banking: within 5 to 7 working days

## If the recharge shows Successful but you didn't get the benefit

1. Restart your phone, or your set-top box for DTH. Operators sometimes take up to 30 minutes to activate a plan.
2. Check the number you recharged in **History**. If you typed a wrong number, the recharge went to that number and can't be reversed.
3. If it's the right number and 2 hours have passed, tap the recharge in **History > Raise an issue**. Kuberix checks with the operator and replies within 48 hours.

## If it's Pending

Wait up to 24 hours. The operator will either confirm it or it fails and you get a refund as above.
