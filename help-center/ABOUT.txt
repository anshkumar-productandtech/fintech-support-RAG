Kuberix Help Center (simulated)

Kuberix is a fictional Indian digital payments company, created for a
portfolio project. It is not a real company and is not affiliated with
any real payments app, bank or fintech.

These 37 help articles (22 for app users, 15 for merchants) were written
with AI assistance (Claude) for this project, in November 2026.

- Kuberix's own policies (fees, limits, settlement times, rewards, support
  hours) are invented for the demo.
- References to the RBI's rules for failed transactions (reversal by T+1
  day for UPI transfers, T+5 days for merchant payments, Rs 100 per day
  compensation for delays) follow RBI circular DPSS.CO.PD No.629/02.01.014/
  2019-20 of September 20, 2019.
- The 1930 helpline and cybercrime.gov.in are India's real National
  Cybercrime reporting channels. cms.rbi.org.in is the RBI's real complaint
  portal.
- Web addresses ending in .example are reserved placeholders and do not
  lead to real sites.

This is not financial, legal or regulatory advice.

Format: one Markdown file per article, with front matter
(id, title, audience, category, last_updated) between two --- lines.
