# Collect payments with payment links

A payment link is a web link you can send by WhatsApp, SMS or email. Your customer opens it and pays with UPI, card, net banking or wallet. You don't need a website.

## Create a link

1. Go to **Payment links > Create**.
2. Enter the amount, a description and the customer's phone or email (optional).
3. Choose when it expires: from 15 minutes to 180 days. The default is **30 days**.
4. Tap **Create**, then **Share**.

## Options

- **Partial payments:** let the customer pay in parts, with a minimum first payment you choose.
- **Reminders:** Kuberix sends up to 3 automatic reminders before the link expires.
- **Custom fields:** ask for things like an order number or address.

## Status of a link

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Issued | Sent, not paid yet |
| Partially paid | Some of the amount paid |
| Paid | Fully paid |
| Expired | Not paid before the expiry time |
| Cancelled | You cancelled it |

Fees are the same as other online payments. See "Fees and charges for merchants".
