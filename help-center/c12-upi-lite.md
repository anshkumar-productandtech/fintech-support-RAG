# UPI Lite: small payments without a PIN

UPI Lite is a small balance stored on your phone for quick, low-value payments. You don't enter your UPI PIN for each payment.

## Limits on Kuberix

- Up to **₹1,000** per payment
- Up to **₹5,000** UPI Lite balance

## Turn it on

1. Go to **Profile > UPI Lite > Activate**.
2. Choose the bank account to load money from.
3. Enter the amount to add and approve with your UPI PIN.

## Good to know

- UPI Lite payments don't show on your bank statement one by one; your bank shows only the top-ups. See each payment in Kuberix **History**.
- You can turn on **Auto top-up** so your balance refills when it drops below an amount you choose.
- If you lose your phone, block your account (see "Lost phone, new phone or changed SIM"). When you set up Kuberix on a new phone, turn off UPI Lite and the balance returns to your bank account within T + 1 day.
- Turning off UPI Lite returns the balance to your linked bank account.
